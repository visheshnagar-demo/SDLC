# Project Features

## SCRUM-280 - Office Visitor Pass System

### Feature Summary
An Office Visitor Pass System where visitors can pre-register visits, employees can approve/reject visitor requests, receptionists can check visitors in and out, and visitor history is maintained.

### Key Features
- Visitor Registration: Allow visitors to pre-register their visits with essential details (name, email, phone, company, purpose, employee visiting).
- Employee Approval: Enable employees to approve or reject visitor requests.
- Receptionist Check-in/Out: Provide receptionists with tools to efficiently manage visitor arrivals and departures.
- Visitor History: Maintain a searchable record of all past visits.
