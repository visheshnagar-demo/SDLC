# Project Features

## SCRUM-324 - Flowers Management System

### Feature Summary
A full-stack Flowers Management System enabling florists and store managers to catalogue flower species, track inventory stock levels, handle supplier details, process customer orders, and view sales analytics.

### Key Features
- Flower Catalog & Inventory Management
- Order Processing & Tracking
- Supplier & Stock Replenishment Alerting
- Dashboard & Sales Analytics
